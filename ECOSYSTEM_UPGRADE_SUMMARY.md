# JobMax Skill-Based Hiring Update

## Deployment 🚀
The updated ecosystem is successfully deployed to production!
**Live Vercel Link:** [https://jobmax-tau.vercel.app](https://jobmax-tau.vercel.app)

---

## What We Built 🛠️

### 1. Developer Portal (Candidate Dashboard)
- **Top Navigation Tabs:** Replaced the simple sub-track switcher with a fully functional top navigation bar spanning four tabs.
- **Skill Profile:** The original radar and role matching view.
- **Explore Contests:** A new view where developers can browse and participate in company-hosted coding contests.
- **Company Projects:** A new view to find real-world company projects to build and prove hands-on skills.
- **Hiring Proposals:** A new view to receive and manage direct job offers from companies based on verified skill matches.
- **Developer Stats Enhancement:** Candidates now display their **GitHub Handle**, **Contest Rating**, and **Projects Completed** right in their header!

### 2. Company Portal (Company Dashboard)
- **Top Navigation Tabs:** Upgraded the company portal to match the multi-view ecosystem.
- **Talent Sourcing:** The original AI Candidate Sourcing view.
- **Skill Contests:** A dedicated view for companies to create, manage, and monitor their technical contests.
- **Company Projects:** A dedicated view for companies to assign, evaluate, and review hands-on projects assigned to candidates.
- **Sent Proposals:** A dashboard to track the status of job proposals sent directly to top-tier candidates.
- **Deep-Dive Update:** When a company views a specific candidate deep-dive modal, they now see the candidate's GitHub Profile link and a direct `Send Job Proposal` button (instead of just 'Technical Screen').

### 3. Landing Page Updates
- **Hero & Philosophy Shift:** Updated the main messaging from "just resume parsing" to "**Don't Just Hire Resumes. Hire Real Skills.**"
- **"How It Works" Visual Journey:** Rewrote the steps to highlight skill profiles, contest participation, github activity, and direct proposals.
- **"I am a..." Selector:** Enhanced the hero by clearly establishing the dual-portal access (Developer vs. Company).
- **Footer Links:** Added actionable routing/modals for links to make the site feel complete.

### 4. Code & Data
- All new features interact natively with `mockEcosystem.js`, meaning the new tabs show context-rich mock data for Contests, Projects, and Proposals. 
- Integrated flawlessly without altering the existing aesthetic or modifying core backend functionality.

Enjoy the new JobMax ecosystem!
